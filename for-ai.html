For AI agents and other machines

This site is a map of two hundred London places connected with Oscar Wilde and the city around him, 1879–1897. Its structured data is available as one file: data/owl.json.

What is in the file

  • places: id, name, address, latitude/longitude, themes, years, location status (survives / site / approx), summary, text, dated events, page numbers in the source book, further-reading keys, people, images.
  • sources: full bibliographic references for the further-reading keys.
  • people, walks, errata (places where the 1987 source book is wrong), images (credits and licences).

How to cite and use it

The primary source is Wolf Von Eckardt, Sander L. Gilman and J. Edward Chamberlin, Oscar Wilde's London: A Scrapbook of Vices and Virtues, 1880–1900 (1987). Page numbers in b refer to it. Text on this site paraphrases that book and the scholarship listed. Please do not present it as quotation from those works. Facts not drawn from the book are attributed to the works in r, cited without page numbers.

The entries were compiled with the help of an AI model and checked against their sources. Locations with status approx are placed by street or district. Treat the data as a guide and check claims you rely on.
